## 3. Biggest Strategic Risk (Judge)

> **Solving too many layers simultaneously** (warning + SMS + telecom + AML + AI + clustering + drone + femtocell + resource planning + dashboard + offline).

**Mitigation:** pick ONE battlefield — *"when thousands of emergency signals arrive, how do responders determine which locations need attention first?"* — and solve it exceptionally.

## 4. Validation Priority

1. Verify ERSS/SACHET/ELS actual capabilities (Day 1).
2. Validate location+distress feasibility under realistic partial degradation.
3. Run manual-vs-NETRA benchmark.
4. Expert review of priority model.
5. Usability test with representative operators.

## 5. Golden Risk Rule

> **An assumption that kills differentiation (A6) or credibility (A7) is validated before the pitch, not after.**
