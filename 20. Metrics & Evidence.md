## 3. What NOT to Claim

- "99.9999% availability" — unless infrastructure supports it
- "Processes millions of events" — unless load-tested
- "AI is 99% accurate" — unless evaluated
- "Works during complete network failure" — unless the communication layer actually supports it
- "NETRA saves thousands of lives" — unsupported

## 4. Evidence Policy

Every reported metric must state:

Dataset
Baseline
Method
