## 1. The 30-Second Pitch (Judge-Validated)

> **"During a major disaster, thousands of distress signals reach authorities through different channels, but responders still face one fundamental problem: which location should they rescue first? NETRA is a disaster intelligence layer that fuses SMS, emergency-service signals, location data and field reports into verified incident clusters. It extracts victim severity and vulnerability, continuously scores rescue priority, and converts those signals into actionable zones and resource recommendations. NETRA doesn't replace 112 or SACHET — it turns their fragmented information into a live operational picture for responders, even when connectivity becomes intermittent."**

## 2. One-Line Version

> **NETRA turns thousands of fragmented emergency signals into a small number of confidence-ranked rescue decisions responders can actually act on.**

## 3. The 3-Word Version

> **Signals → Decisions → Rescue**

## 4. Problem (5 Seconds)

Fragmented, duplicated, uncertain distress signals overwhelm responders during disasters — deciding where to rescue first is the hard problem.

## 5. Solution (5 Seconds)

An intelligence layer that fuses multi-source evidence into confidence-weighted, severity-ranked rescue zones with explainable resource recommendations.

## 6. Why Now / Why Us

- Existing channels (112, SMS, ELS) already generate the signals; nobody consolidates them into prioritized operations.
- AI is used only where it materially helps: multilingual unstructured extraction.
- The core is a transparent Rescue Priority Score, not black-box magic.
- Human-in-the-loop: we recommend, responders decide.

## 7. Differentiation (10 Seconds)

```text
SACHET warns. ERSS responds. ELS locates.
NETRA fuses evidence and prioritizes rescue decisions.
```

We are not another map. We are the decision layer.

## 8. Evidence (10 Seconds)

- benchmark: triage time manual ~8 min vs NETRA ~22 s (measured)
- 1,000 reports → ~75 prioritized incidents
- multilingual extraction precision/recall/F1 on labelled dataset
- adversarial robustness: fake-SOS bursts detected, confidence reduced

## 9. Demo (30 Seconds)

Live simulated flood: 50 reports in → 17 incidents → 6 zones → 2 P1; field verification re-prioritizes; RESCUED moves off the active queue. Deterministic, offline, recoverable.

## 10. What We Are NOT Claiming

- not working during complete cellular blackout
- not replacing 112 / SACHET
- not autonomous dispatch
- not "AI saves lives"

## 11. Closing Line

> **"The reason NETRA can win is not that it uses AI, SMS or a map — it's that it converts thousands of fragmented emergency signals into a small number of confidence-ranked rescue decisions that responders can act on."**

## 12. Golden Pitch Rule

> **Never pitch the technology. Pitch the decision problem and the measured improvement.**
